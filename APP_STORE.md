# App Store Visual Direction

## Working metadata

**Title:** IQ Games: Brain Challenge  
**Subtitle:** Logic Games & Daily Puzzles

## Screenshot sequence

### 1. CAN YOU SOLVE THIS?
Hero mechanic: One Move  
Support line: **One move. Make all three towers equal.**

This should be the first screenshot because the challenge is understandable in less than a second and the player can mentally start solving before reading anything else.

### 2. FOLD IT IN YOUR MIND
Hero mechanic: Fold  
Support line: **Two folds. One punch. Picture the result.**

This demonstrates that the game is not just number sequences or trivia. It communicates spatial reasoning and tactile interaction.

### 3. 7 GAMES. ONE BRAIN.
Hero screen: Daily IQ Run  
Support line: **A fresh mix of quick challenges every day.**

Show Strategy, Spatial, Focus, Logic, Memory, Pattern, and Final Challenge as a varied daily session.

## Icon direction

The current icon is intentionally light-first: warm ivory background, premium violet Q mark, a small gold accent, and a soft mint secondary accent. It is designed to remain recognizable at small App Store sizes without relying on text.

Source asset: `assets/app-icon.svg`

## Capture helpers

The main prototype supports clean preview states through query parameters:

- `?preview=home`
- `?preview=onemove`
- `?preview=fold`
- `?preview=balance`
- `?preview=path`

These are intended for later screenshot capture and design review.

## Claim policy

Do **not** use fabricated claims such as “Only 8% solve this,” fake player counts, or population percentile rankings. Any competitive/statistical claim should come from real production data.
